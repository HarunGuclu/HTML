MY AUTOBIOGRAPHY

Hello My Name is Harun

I was born on May 12, 2002. I was born in Bursa. I am a 12th grade student at an Anatolian High School. I study in the numerical department. I graduated from primary school as the top student.
Although I was in an Anatolian High School and in the numerical department, I have always been interested in the Fine Arts High School or the verbal department. I won first place in a poetry competition held at school during my primary school years. Then I tried writing novels and essays for a long time.
I wrote articles for a website for 3 months during the last summer vacation. Unfortunately, I quit for some reasons. I have a curious and adventurous personality who is sensitive to the events around me, loves to research, and thinks that a person grows a little more with each piece of information they learn, not with time.
I have a character that immediately notices innovations around me and tries to learn this innovation by doing research on the subject. I look at events first from my own perspective, then from the perspective of the other person and finally from the perspective of a person watching from the outside.
Thanks to my nature that hates making mistakes and never forgets mistakes, I learn not to make mistakes over time. I love my character as someone who loves talking to people, answers questions with great pleasure, does research on medicine, follows fashion closely, and is in the middle of a great battle to be the best.
I am an 18-year-old high school student who aims to be an oncologist, who is extremely selfish, has a practical mind that tries to solve the problem she encounters in her own way, is responsible, and never defends her knowledge of a subject she does not know. I am passionate about writing.
